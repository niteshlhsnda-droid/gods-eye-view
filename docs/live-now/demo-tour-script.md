# "Play today's tour" — 60-second demo narration script

ChatGPT SELL lane, 2026-10-13.

## Rules this script follows

- ~150 spoken words total (≈60s at natural pace). Timings are presenter
  pacing, not hard cuts.
- **Only verified/reported events.** Unconfirmed events NEVER enter the
  auto-tour (§4.5) — this script names none.
- Presenter names only verified facts: names, numbers, places, times.
  No casualty speculation on aviation/maritime events.
- Each line starts with a **[camera cue]** so the presenter syncs with the
  tour; the tour dwells ~3s per event per spec.

## Script (3 sample events)

**[0:00 — globe spinning up, Live Now panel open]**
This is the planet, live. In the last 24 hours, the world's sensors flagged
three events worth your eyes.

**[0:08 — fly to Bay of Bengal, cyclone marker pulsing]**
First: Cyclone Arna, Category 3, 185 kilometres an hour, bearing down on
the Bay of Bengal coast. Landfall expected within the day — verified by two
weather agencies.

**[0:23 — fly to Chile coast, quake marker]**
Second: a magnitude 6.2 earthquake off the coast of Chile, 10 kilometres
deep, twenty minutes ago. Shaking reports are still coming in.

**[0:38 — fly to launch site, space marker]**
And third: a Falcon 9 launch out of Cape Canaveral, live tracking on the
ascent trajectory right now.

**[0:50 — pull back to full globe]**
Open Live Now. See the planet breathe. Fly anywhere, in under two seconds.
That's your day, from orbit — every day.

## Presenter notes

- If a slot has no verified event, the tour builder skips it (never fill
  with an unconfirmed item — say "the planet's quiet on that front").
- Severity-5 aviation/maritime: state flight/vessel, squawk, position,
  time. Full stop. If asked about casualties on air: "Live Now reports what
  sensors see — verified facts only."
- Read confidence labels aloud when visible ("this one's reported, not
  verified yet") — it builds trust, not doubt.
